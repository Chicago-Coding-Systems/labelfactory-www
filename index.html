Printing on the floor

  1. Install the agent

    Put the Label Factory Agent on a Windows PC near your printers. It connects out to Label Factory, so there's nothing to open in your firewall.

  2. See your printers

    The PC's printers show up in Label Factory. Zebra printers get ZPL directly. Everything else prints through its Windows driver.

  3. Print

    Operators open the Print page, fill in the form or scan an item number, and print. They never see the design, so they can't change it.

Your ERP or line controller can print the same labels through the API, with an API key.
